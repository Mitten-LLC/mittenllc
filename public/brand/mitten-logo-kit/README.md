# Mitten logo kit

Approved palette:

- Midnight navy: `#071D3A`
- Maize gold: `#FFCB05`
- Ice blue: `#DBE1FF`
- Warm white: `#FFFEF9`

## Website

- `web/mitten-wordmark-primary.svg` — default header and footer wordmark.
- `web/mitten-wordmark-reversed.svg` — for navy or dark backgrounds.
- `web/mitten-mark-compact.svg` — compact navigation, profile, and app mark.
- `web/mitten-favicon.svg` — simplified compact mark sized for browser tabs.

## Email

- `email/mitten-email-wordmark.svg` and `.png` — 180 × 46 px signature logo.
- `email/mitten-email-mark.svg` and `.png` — 48 × 48 px compact signature mark.

PNG is recommended in email signatures because support for SVG varies by mail client.

## Official documents

- `documents/mitten-document-wordmark.svg` and `.png` — 360 × 92 px letterhead wordmark.
- `documents/mitten-document-mark.svg` and `.png` — 96 × 96 px cover/footer mark.
- `documents/mitten-document-monochrome.svg` and `.png` — one-color version for black-and-white printing.

Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least the width of the gold `i` dot. Do not recolor, stretch, rotate, or rearrange the mark.
